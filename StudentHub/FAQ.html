Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions asked by StudentHub users.

1. Why can't I log in to my account?

Make sure your username/email and password are correct. Check whether Caps Lock is enabled, and use the Forgot Password option if needed.

2. Why is the website loading slowly?

This may be caused by a slow internet connection, browser cache, or high server traffic. Try refreshing the page or using another browser.

3. Why am I not receiving the verification email?

Check your Spam/Junk folder, verify your email address, and request another verification email.

4. Why can't I upload a file?

Ensure your file meets the required size and format. Also check your internet connection before trying again.

5. What should I do if the website is not opening?

Check your internet connection, verify the website address, or try opening the website in another browser or device.
